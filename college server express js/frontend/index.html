VICE CHANCELLOR’S MESSAGE

CBLU,Bhiwani was established by the government of Haryana in 2014 to expand the access of people to 
quality education and ensure equity.It is a fast growing institute making all endeavours to grow as 
a centre for knowledge creation and dissemination with a thrust on sports and physical education,
skill development and, extension and co-curricular activities activities to contribute meaningfully 
to building Atmnirbhar Bharat. 

Indigenous research agenda,students’ welfare, balancing quantitative and qualitative academic growth, 
promoting diversity,developing new and updating educational programmes, and intruding industry interface 
have been identified as priority areas to work on.Conducive teaching-learning environment, new academic 
programmes, sports achievements and quality research output make this university a good choice for the 
students.
